How the implied value is built

implied = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × chains

The multiple is earned, not assumed. For each category it is the median FDV/TVL of the live-token protocols in that same category — ratios winsorized to 0.02–3.0, clamped to 0.05–1.0. A category with fewer than three live comps falls back to the market-wide median (—). Growth is the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50…+100% and weighted 0.6; maturity rises from 0.6 for a brand-new listing to 1.0 at three years; each extra chain adds 2%, up to five. Excluded from the board: exchanges, versioned pools (Aave V3 and the like), protocols whose name contains wrapped, staked or staking, and the derivative categories — Bridge, Canonical Bridge, Cross Chain Bridge, Liquid Staking, Liquid Restaking, Restaked BTC and Stablecoin Wrapper. The forty watchlist names are ranked as the brief requires, whatever their category.
